# Third-party assets

Every third-party asset in this repo gets a row here before it lands: source
URL, author, licence, and the date it was downloaded. CC0 preferred; CC BY is
fine as long as the attribution is recorded. See CLAUDE.md.

| Asset | Path | Source URL | Author | Licence | Downloaded |
|-------|------|------------|--------|---------|------------|
| Kenney Racing Kit — `raceCarRed` | `public/models/raceCarRed.glb` | https://kenney.nl/assets/racing-kit | Kenney (kenney.nl) | CC0 1.0 | 2026-09-18 |

Through M2 the scene was procedural geometry only — the track ribbon and
markers are `BufferGeometry` built from `public/tracks/monza.json`, and the
corner labels are DOM text in the system monospace stack rather than a bundled
font. The first asset arrived with the car model in M4; M3's car was a box.

## Derived data

Not an asset in the sense above (no model, texture, HDRI or font), but
`public/tracks/monza.json` is derived from third-party data and its provenance
belongs somewhere, so: it is generated by `scripts/extract_track.py` from
FastF1 position telemetry for the 2024 Italian Grand Prix, extracted
2026-09-18. FastF1 itself is MIT-licensed. Corner marker positions come via
FastF1's `get_circuit_info()`, sourced from MultiViewer
(https://multiviewer.app/). Corner *names* are written by hand in
`CORNER_NAMES` and are descriptive geographic names, not F1 marks.
